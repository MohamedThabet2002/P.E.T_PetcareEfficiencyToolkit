Petcare Efficiency Toolkit (P.E.T) - What's new in this release
================================================================

2.0.0-beta1
  - Full in-place upgrade path: existing v1.0.0 databases are archived and
    migrated automatically, with no data loss.
  - Reports center overhaul: a period bar with presets, a navigator calendar,
    click-to-save report history (open / compare / export), and daily auto
    checkpoints.
  - RBAC: editable role templates and per-user permission overrides, with
    custom roles and full permission catalog.
  - Record-book clinical exam (9 tables) in the Add Visit wizard.
  - Undo/restore for deleted owners, pets, visits and appointments.
  - Excel / CSV import & export plus templates from Settings > Database Backup.
  - Stock batches with expiry date, buy price and per-batch receipt linking.
  - Built-in backup encryption, audit trail on every add / edit / delete.
  - New installer: bilingual (English / Arabic), branded artwork, and
    preservation of your data on upgrades.
